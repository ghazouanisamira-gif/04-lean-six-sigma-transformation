# Recruitment Control Plan

## Objective

Sustain the improvement achieved through the Lean Six Sigma project.

| Control | KPI / Measure | Frequency | Owner | Trigger | Response |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Recruitment dashboard | Early attrition / recruitment KPIs | Monthly | Recruitment | Performance gap | Root cause review |
| Process review | Process compliance | Monthly | Process Owner | Deviation | Corrective action |
| Candidate flow review | Conversion / pipeline | Weekly | Recruitment | Drop-off | Process analysis |
| Early-stage review | Attrition / onboarding indicators | Monthly | Recruitment + Operations | Risk signal | Retention action |
| Governance review | Overall performance | Monthly | Management | KPI deviation | Escalation / action |

## Sustainability Mechanisms

- Standardized procedures
- KPI ownership
- Dashboard monitoring
- Management reviews
- Corrective actions
- Continuous improvement
