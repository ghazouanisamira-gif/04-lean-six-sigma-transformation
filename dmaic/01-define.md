# DMAIC — Define

## Problem Statement

Define the operational problem in clear, measurable terms and establish why it matters to the organization.

## Project Charter Elements

- Business problem
- Project objective
- Scope
- Stakeholders
- Expected benefits
- Constraints and assumptions

## Critical Questions

1. What problem are we solving?
2. Who is affected?
3. What is the expected business impact?
4. What is in scope and out of scope?
5. How will success be measured?

## Output

A clear project charter and agreed problem statement that establish the foundation for the remaining DMAIC phases.
