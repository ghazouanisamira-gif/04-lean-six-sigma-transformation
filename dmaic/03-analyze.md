# DMAIC — Analyze

## Objective

Identify and validate the root causes that contribute to the performance gap.

## Analysis Toolkit

### Pareto Analysis

Prioritize the causes that contribute most significantly to the observed problem.

### Cause & Effect Analysis

Structure potential causes across relevant categories such as people, process, tools, environment and management.

### Value Stream Mapping

Identify delays, handoffs, rework, waiting time and non-value-added activities.

### Chi-Square Analysis

Assess whether categorical variables show evidence of association when appropriate.

### Logistic Regression

Explore how relevant variables may relate to the probability of an outcome such as early attrition, when the data and assumptions support the analysis.

## Root Cause Validation

Potential causes should be supported by evidence rather than assumptions.

| Potential Cause | Evidence | Validated? | Priority |
|---|---|---|---|
| | | Yes / No | High / Medium / Low |

## Output

A prioritized set of validated root causes that can be addressed during the Improve phase.
