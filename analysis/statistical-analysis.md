# Statistical Analysis

## Objective

Use data to assess relationships between recruitment characteristics and early employee attrition.

## Techniques

### Chi-Square Analysis

Used where appropriate to examine associations between categorical variables.

### Logistic Regression

Used to explore how relevant variables may relate to the probability of early attrition.

## Analytical Principle

Statistical methods were used as decision-support tools within the DMAIC process.

The objective was not to replace operational judgement, but to strengthen root-cause validation with evidence.

## Analytical Flow

**Define Y → Identify X Variables → Validate Data → Analyze Relationships → Interpret → Act**
