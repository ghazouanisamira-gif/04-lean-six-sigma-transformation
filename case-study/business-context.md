# Business Context

## Industry

**Customer Contact Center / Business Process Outsourcing (BPO)**

## Operational Scale

- Operational workforce: **~250 agents**
- Annual recruitment volume: **~100**
- Environment: people-intensive, multi-client customer operations

## Context

The organization maintained a relatively stable operational workforce while recruiting regularly to compensate for structural employee departures.

The project therefore focused on improving the **quality and sustainability of recruitment**, rather than attempting to eliminate structural turnover inherent to the Contact Center environment.

## Key Performance Issue

A significant proportion of new employees were leaving within the first six months.

### Baseline

**Early attrition (<6 months): 28%**

### Project Objective

Reduce early attrition to **below 15%** through recruitment process improvement, stronger selection practices, structured governance and better early-stage monitoring.

## Why the Problem Mattered

Early departures created:

- Replacement recruitment requirements
- Additional onboarding effort
- Workforce instability
- Pressure on operational capacity
- Risk to service-level commitments
- Reduced recruitment efficiency

## Transformation Perspective

The project treated recruitment as an end-to-end business process:

**Sourcing → Screening → Selection → Onboarding Alignment → Early Retention → Performance Monitoring**

The objective was to make this process measurable, structured and continuously improvable.
