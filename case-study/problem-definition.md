# Problem Definition

## Problem Statement

Early employee attrition was identified as a significant performance issue within a high-volume Contact Center recruitment environment.

At baseline, **28% of new recruits left the organization before completing six months**.

The project focused on identifying the process and recruitment factors that could be influenced to improve early retention.

## Three Structural Problems

### 1. Organizational Alignment

Recruitment resources, responsibilities and operating practices were not sufficiently aligned with the required recruitment volume and quality expectations.

### 2. Data & Governance

The recruitment function did not have a sufficiently structured system for:

- KPI monitoring
- Candidate tracking
- Performance reporting
- Cross-functional coordination
- Continuous improvement

### 3. Operational Delivery

Recruitment performance was not consistently aligned with operational workforce requirements and service commitments.

## Critical Question

The project therefore asked:

> Which recruitment-process factors are contributing to early employee attrition, and how can the process be redesigned to improve recruitment quality and workforce stability?

## Project Y

**Y = Early employee attrition within the first six months**

## Target

**<15% early attrition**
