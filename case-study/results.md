# Results

## Main Result

Early employee attrition was reduced from:

**28% → 10%**

This represents a relative reduction of approximately:

**−64%**

The initial project objective of reducing early attrition below 15% was therefore achieved.

## Operational Impact

At the baseline recruitment volume, the improvement represented approximately **18 fewer early departures per year**.

The project also contributed to stronger operational workforce reliability and improved alignment between recruitment capacity and operational requirements.

## SLA Performance

Operational service-level compliance improved to:

**100%**

compared with a previously observed range of approximately **60–70%**.

## Business Impact Areas

- Reduced early replacement requirements
- Improved recruitment quality
- Greater workforce stability
- Better process visibility
- Stronger governance
- Improved operational alignment
- More structured performance management

## Financial Impact

The original Black Belt project included a detailed ROI model covering recruitment replacement costs, sourcing optimization and organizational restructuring.

For confidentiality reasons, detailed salary data, sourcing costs, internal cost assumptions and financial calculations are not published in this repository.

The public case study therefore focuses on the **validated operational outcomes and transformation methodology**.

## Sustainability

The improvement was supported by:

- Standardized recruitment processes
- KPI monitoring
- Management dashboards
- Clearer process ownership
- Control routines
- Continuous improvement mechanisms
