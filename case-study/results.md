# Results

## Main Result

Early employee attrition was reduced from:

**28% → 10%**

This represents an approximate:

**−64% relative reduction**

The original project target was **below 15%**, which was reached.

## Operational Interpretation

At the baseline recruitment volume, the improvement represented approximately **18 fewer early departures per year**.

The transformation also supported stronger workforce reliability and improved alignment between recruitment activity and operational requirements.

## Additional Performance Result

Operational SLA compliance reached:

**100%**

compared with a previously observed range of approximately **60–70%**.

## What Changed

The result was supported by a combination of:

- process redesign
- improved screening and selection
- clearer responsibilities
- standardized workflows
- stronger Recruitment–Operations coordination
- KPI implementation
- performance dashboards
- structured management routines
- control mechanisms

The result should therefore be understood as a **system-level improvement**, not as the effect of one isolated action.

## Financial Impact

The original Black Belt project included internal financial modelling.

Detailed salary data, sourcing costs, replacement-cost assumptions and ROI calculations are intentionally excluded from the public repository.

## Sustainability

The control approach included:

- standardized recruitment procedures
- KPI monitoring
- management dashboards
- process ownership
- periodic reviews
- corrective action tracking
- continuous improvement

## Portfolio Principle

**Show the thinking, not the confidential playbook.**

The public case exposes the problem, method, analytical reasoning and validated outcome while protecting sensitive operational data.
