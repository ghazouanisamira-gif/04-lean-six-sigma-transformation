# Project Objectives

## Project Period

**December 2023 – January 2026**

## Primary Objective

Redesign the recruitment process and reduce early employee attrition through a structured Lean Six Sigma transformation.

## Objectives

1. Establish a reliable recruitment performance baseline.
2. Map the end-to-end recruitment flow.
3. Identify and validate root causes of early attrition.
4. Redesign critical recruitment activities.
5. Clarify roles, responsibilities and governance.
6. Introduce structured recruitment KPIs.
7. Improve Recruitment–Operations alignment.
8. Establish a sustainable control framework.
9. Structure data for future automation and AI opportunities.

## Main Project Y

**Early employee attrition within the first six months**

## Success Criteria

| Measure | Baseline | Target | Result |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Early attrition (<6 months) | 28% | <15% | **10%** |
| Relative reduction | — | — | **−64%** |

## Scope

### Included

- Recruitment workflow
- Candidate screening and selection
- Recruitment governance
- Recruitment performance measurement
- Stakeholder handoffs
- Onboarding alignment
- Early-stage retention monitoring

### Excluded

- Structural turnover unrelated to recruitment quality
- Individual employee performance management outside the defined project scope
- Confidential financial assumptions

## Success Logic

**Reduce the measurable performance gap → improve the process → establish controls → sustain the result.**
