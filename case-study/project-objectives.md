# Project Objectives

## Primary Objective

Improve the recruitment process and reduce early employee attrition through a structured Lean Six Sigma approach.

## Objectives

1. Establish a reliable recruitment performance baseline.
2. Map and analyze the current recruitment process.
3. Identify and validate root causes of early attrition.
4. Redesign critical recruitment activities.
5. Clarify roles, responsibilities and governance.
6. Introduce structured recruitment KPIs.
7. Improve alignment between Recruitment and Operations.
8. Establish a sustainable control framework.
9. Strengthen data availability for future automation and AI opportunities.

## Success Criteria

| Measure | Baseline | Objective | Result |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Early attrition (<6 months) | 28% | <15% | **10%** |
| Relative improvement | — | — | **−64%** |
