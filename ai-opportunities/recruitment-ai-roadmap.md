# Recruitment AI Roadmap

## Strategic Context

The Lean Six Sigma transformation created a more structured recruitment process and improved the availability of process data.

This creates a foundation for evaluating future automation and AI opportunities.

## Strategic Sequence

**Standardize → Structure Data → Measure → Automate → Augment with AI**

## Potential Use Cases

### 1. AI-Assisted Candidate Screening

Automated first-level screening based on predefined job criteria.

### 2. Candidate Pre-Qualification

Conversational tools could collect basic candidate information and support faster qualification.

### 3. Multi-Channel Flow Management

An ATS or recruitment platform could consolidate candidate flows across sourcing channels.

### 4. Profile / Job Matching

NLP-based analysis could support structured comparison between candidate profiles and job requirements.

### 5. Recruitment Content

Generative AI could support the creation and adaptation of recruitment content across channels.

### 6. Early-Attrition Monitoring

Predictive analytics could be explored to identify early warning signals during the first months of employment.

## Important Limitation

These use cases represent **future-state opportunities and feasibility hypotheses** identified through the project.

They should not be interpreted as fully implemented AI solutions.

Any future deployment would require appropriate data governance, validation, human oversight, bias controls and business testing.
